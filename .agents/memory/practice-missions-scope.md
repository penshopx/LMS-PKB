Adapt the workplace-mission concept from microlearning-7.replit.app as supplemental practice, not a replacement for the existing course and post-test flow.

**Why:** The reference mixes workplace coaching with marketplace, AI-generation, and formal SKK/BNSP claims. The chosen LMS scope is organization-led learning with course-completion certificates only. Missions should provide practical feedback without silently changing agreed graduation criteria.

**How to apply:** Keep practice rounds, evidence, feedback, and repetition separate from lesson completion, quiz-attempt quotas, and certificate eligibility. Admins author and review; PICs monitor their own BUJK read-only and never receive private reflections. Do not introduce AI claims, PKB credit, or formal competency verification merely because the reference markets them.
