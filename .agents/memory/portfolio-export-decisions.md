---
name: Portfolio export decisions
description: Rationale for portable preparation documents and conservative classification of historical data.
---

Use a portable, self-contained preparation document rather than treating an export as a ready-to-submit official application.

**Why:** The task requests help preparing an official submission, not an integration with an official submission service. Printable HTML avoids adding a PDF service or dependency and lets participants retain a readable local copy. Actual evidence files may not exist in application storage, so a document must not imply they have been bundled or verified.

**How to apply:** Preserve readable download and browser-print behavior when extending formats. New evidence packaging or official submission workflows require separate scope.

When historical references are missing or combine old and active curricula, preserve uncertainty instead of attributing them to active PKB.

**Why:** Treating an unknown record or mixed learning path as active PKB would change its meaning and could mislead a participant preparing a submission.

**How to apply:** Retain unclassified records visibly; never infer credit eligibility from a course title, progress value, or internal review.
